Privacy Policy — Clear Daily Planner

Effective 14 September 2026 · Clear ("the app") is published by Ender Tolunay.

A private place to plan

Clear can be used without an account. Without one, your plans, routines, check-ins, and settings are saved only on your device. Clear contains no advertising and no analytics SDK. We do not collect usage analytics, we do not track you, and we never sell data.

Optional account and sync

If you choose to sign in (Apple or Google), a Supabase Auth account is created. Supabase receives your account identifier and available profile information, such as your email or Apple relay email. Signed-in tasks, notes and attached photos, subtasks, routines, and check-ins are stored in Supabase PostgreSQL in the United States (us-east-2) and synchronized between your devices. Transport is encrypted; storage is not end-to-end encrypted. Access is restricted to your account with database ownership checks and row-level security.

What stays on the device

Calendar permissions and events, imported reminder identifiers, notification preferences, onboarding settings, and focus timers stay local. Calendar access is used only to display events; Clear never edits your calendars. Reminders imports are copies you review first; Clear never writes back to Apple Reminders. The photo picker shares only images you select, and Clear stores optimized copies without original photo metadata.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by the App Store and remain associated with your Apple Account. Clear does not see or store your payment details.

On-device intelligence

Optional planning assistance runs on-device with Apple Intelligence on supported devices. Task text is not sent to an external AI server.

Notifications

All reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push-notification service receives your data.

Your controls

Export or restore backups in Settings. Widgets and Live Activities may show task titles on your Home or Lock Screen; manage them in iOS Settings. Signing out keeps your local plans. Account deletion removes the authenticated account and its cloud planning records; a minimal deletion marker (former account ID and a deletion flag, no email or planning content) remains to block delayed uploads. Devices that were offline and backups you exported may retain copies until you remove them.

Children

Clear is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact
